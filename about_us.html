ABOUT US

FRC4617 DAUN, aims to provide girls in grades 9-12 the opportunity to participate in FIRST Robotics in a setting as part of an all-girls team. experience to approach challenges in innovative and creative manners and to inspire them with all aspects of science, technology and leadership.

FRC4617 DAUN is unique experience for girls wishing to participate in FIRST Robotics Competition. The team is hosted by, WE-FIRST, an Undergraduate Engineering student club in the Faculty of Engineering at Western Univervisty. The WE-FIRST club has undergraduate engineering student who were dedicated participants in FRC teams during high-school. They recognize the value FRC had in shaping their career goals and interest in STEM fields and want to give back as mentors. With support and guidance of faculty and staff advisors. WE-FIRST hosts and mentors FRC4617 DAUN.
