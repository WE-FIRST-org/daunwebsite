ABOUT US

FRC4617 DAUN, aims to provide girls in grades 9-12 the opportunity to participate in FIRST Robotics in a setting as part of an all-girls team. experience to approach challenges in innovative and creative manners and to inspire them with all aspects of science, technology and leadership.


Based in London, Ontario, FRC Team 4617 (DAUN) is the city's first and only all-girls robotics team. With Western University as a support and anchor for the team, DAUN members collectively brainstorm innovative ways to solve modern problems, develop new technical and transferable skills, and nurture their passion for robotics and engineering. Each year, DAUN builds a robot that follows the prompts and challenges posted by For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology (FIRST) to compete in their annual FIRST Robotics Competition.

FRC4617 DAUN is unique experience for girls wishing to participate in FRC. The team is hosted by WE-FIRST, an Undergraduate Engineering student club in the Faculty of Engineering at Western University.


The WE-FIRST club has undergraduate engineering students who were dedicated participants in FRC teams during high-school. They recognize the value FRC had in shaping their career goals and interest in STEM fields and want to give back as mentors with support and guidance of faculty and staff advisors. WE-FIRST hosts and mentors FRC4617 DAUN.

Build connections! Members of DAUN create close interpersonal and lifelong friendships with their fellow teammates. Meeting regularly and working together on various tachnical problems and creating a robot creates the perfect environment to bond with others with similar interests and passions.
